Evidence note on breathing: slow breathing near 6 breaths/min has strong support
([meta-analysis of 223 studies](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0149763422002007)). Box breathing's
evidence is thin, and one trial found it [raised post-exercise heart rate](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12622787/).

## Safety layer to add (from the sources)

1. **Pain guide (NHSi-BP):** rate pain 0–10; keep it at 5 or below; stop if an exercise causes new
   pain or makes symptoms worse.
2. **Per-exercise cautions** shown in the player: knee-to-chest (osteoporosis), legs up the wall
   (glaucoma, heart problems), child's pose (pain down a leg → stop).
3. **One-time note at first launch:** not medical advice; check with a doctor if you have a heart
   condition, recent surgery, are pregnant, or have unexplained pain.
4. **"Source: NHS / Mayo Clinic"** line under each exercise, linking to the page.

## Still recommended

A one-time review by a chartered physiotherapist of the final 29 before launch — the only thing that
makes "verified" a true claim. The two ⚠️ items are for them to decide.
