Exercise figures — review

Each row: start · moving · next pose · moving back.

Slow belly breathing ex-regulate-1 · source: NHS

Sit or stand comfortably and loosen anything tight · Breathe in gently through your nose for a count of 5 · Breathe out through your mouth for a count of 5 · Let the breath reach your belly without forcing it — it's fine not to reach 5 at first

Bridge ex-strengthen-2 · source: Mayo Clinic

Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat · Tighten your tummy and bottom muscles · Lift your hips until your knees, hips and shoulders make a straight line · Hold for three slow breaths, then lower. Rest between lifts

Wall press-up ex-strengthen-3 · source: NHS

Stand an arm’s length from a wall, hands on it at chest height · Keeping your back straight, bend your arms to bring your chest towards the wall · Push back to the start · Do 5 slowly, rest, and repeat while the timer runs
